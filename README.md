# final-project
Everyone talks about the importance of branding these days. A crucial part of "branding" is having a unique, personal style that other people can distinguish you by. If someone were to turn to the Internet or social media, they can find outfit/hairstyle/jewelry inspiration for any general style, but it is difficult to determine what style is truly right for them. My quiz seeks to solve that problem by helping people determine three crucial elements of their personal brand: colors, jewelry styles, and clothing styles based on the most important factors. Colors should be primarily determined by which palette best suits someone's natural coloring (including hair color, skin undertones, and eye color) while jewelry styles should be based on someone's lifestyle preferences as well as stylistic choices. Clothing style, however, should serve to reflect someone's personality, which is why that component of my quiz is based on someone's interests and selects one of a few general styles that best encapsulates them. Together, the goal of this quiz is to provide the user with a clothing style to guide their outfit inspiration as well as recommendations and comfortability preferences. on how to personalize it with colors and accessories that will make them feel most comfortable while best complementing their physical features.

Changes since FP Status: I added a few more questions to further specify the jewelry section as well as add some questions to the clothing section that gets at stylistic preferences the user might already have. I also removed the random library, so now if there is a tie, both options get printed instead of just one. The user also has the option to see their full breakdown of points in each category if they would like more expansive recommendations.

As the code instructs, you simply enter the number choice of the option for each question, and then you get your results based on your input at the end of the quiz!
