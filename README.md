# final-project
Everyone talks about branding these days. A crucial part of "branding" is having a unique, personal style that other people can distinguish you by. If someone were to turn to the Internet or social media, they can find outfit/hairstyle/jewelry inspiration for any general style, but it is difficult to determine what style is truly right for them. My quiz seeks to solve that problem by helping people determine three crucial elements of their personal brand: colors, jewelry styles, and clothing styles based on the most important factors. Colors should be primarily determined by which palette best suits someone's natural coloring (including hair color, skin undertones, and eye color) while jewelry styles should be based on someone's lifestyle preferences as well as stylistic choices. Clothing style, however, should serve to reflect someone's personality, which is why that component of my quiz is based on someone's interests and selects one of a few general styles that best encapsulates them. Together, the goal of this quiz is to provide the user with a clothing style to guide their outfit inspiration as well as recommendations on how to personalize it with colors and accessories that will best complement their physical features
The goal of my code now is to act as a buzzfeed-like quiz that helps someone determine a unique, personal style based on a color palette that best complements their coloring, a jewelry type that suits their preferences and lifestyle, and a clothing type based on their personality and tastes. As the code instructs, you simply enter the number choice of the option for each question, and then you get your results based on your input at the end of the quiz!
